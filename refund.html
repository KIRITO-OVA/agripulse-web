Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: February 05, 2026

1. Digital Service Policy

AgriPulse Analytics provides digital market intelligence. Due to the immediate delivery of digital goods (API response), we generally do not offer refunds once the data has been successfully transmitted.

2. Cancellation

For recurring subscription plans, users may cancel their subscription at any time via the user portal. Cancellations will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No partial refunds are provided for the remaining duration.

3. Error-Based Refunds

In the event of a technical failure on our part (e.g., payment processed but API key not activated), please contact support within 24 hours for a resolution or a full refund.
